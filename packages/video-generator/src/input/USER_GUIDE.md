# Sunshine Dental

An AI-powered front desk for dental clinics — chat and voice receptionists, scheduling, and encrypted patient records in one calm app.

## Dashboard
<!-- route: / -->
Staff sign in to a calm dashboard showing today's appointments, new patients, and clinic activity at a glance.

## Calendar
<!-- route: /calendar -->
The calendar shows every provider's schedule side by side, so the front desk can spot free slots and plan the day in seconds.

## Patients
<!-- route: /patients -->
Patient records live in one searchable list — contact details and history, with personal data encrypted at rest.

## Appointments
<!-- route: /appointments -->
Appointments can be created, confirmed, and rescheduled in a couple of clicks, with statuses that keep the whole team in sync.

## Call Logs
<!-- route: /call-logs -->
Calls handled by the AI voice receptionist are logged with transcripts and outcomes, ready for the team to review.

## Chat Logs
<!-- route: /chat-logs -->
Every chat conversation is stored too — staff can audit what the assistant said and follow up when needed.

## Settings
<!-- route: /settings -->
Settings hold clinic hours, appointment types, and preferences — and the whole app speaks English, Hungarian, and German.
