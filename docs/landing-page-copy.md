## Objection-handling / FAQ section

**Does the AI replace my staff?**
No. It handles repetitive phone work so your team can focus on patients. Your staff stays fully in control.

**What if the AI can't handle a call?**
It captures the request and logs a callback so a human can follow up. You see everything in the Call Logs.

**Will it double-book us?**
Never. It books only from your live calendar, so it only offers slots that are genuinely free.

**Can I see what the AI told a patient?**
Yes — every call has a full transcript and summary.

**Is patient information private?**
Yes. Access is restricted by role, accounts are password-protected, and only your authorized staff can log in.
