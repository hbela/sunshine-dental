# SaaS Subscription Agreement — Template

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT — READ FIRST**
> This is a **plain-language starting template**, not legal advice. It exists so you can negotiate sensibly and hand a qualified lawyer a 70%-done draft instead of a blank page.
>
> **Before using this with a real customer, have a lawyer qualified in your jurisdiction review it.** Three reasons this matters more than usual here:
> 1. You process patient **personal data plus call recordings and transcripts**. The clinic's lawful basis for the booking data is normally **contractual necessity / legitimate interest / a healthcare legal obligation** (not blanket explicit consent), but recordings, transcripts, and any health-related details are **sensitive**, so a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** is mandatory, not optional. (See `compliance.md`.)
> 2. Patients must be told, **on the call itself**, that they're speaking to an **AI assistant** and that the call may be **recorded** — this is a legal/transparency requirement, not just good manners.
> 3. The AI books appointments and talks to patients on the clinic's behalf — you need clear **liability limits** and an **"AI is not perfect" disclaimer**.
>
> Fill in every `[BRACKETED]` field. Delete this warning block before sending.

## Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement

**This Agreement** is entered into on `[DATE]` between:

- **Provider:** `[YOUR COMPANY NAME]`, `[legal form, e.g. Kft. / Ltd.]`, registered at `[ADDRESS]`, company no. `[REG NO]` ("we", "us", "Provider"); and
- **Customer:** `[CLINIC LEGAL NAME]`, registered at `[ADDRESS]`, company no. `[REG NO]` ("you", "Customer").

Together, the "Parties."

---

### 1. The Service
1.1. We provide **Sunshine Dental**, a software service consisting of: (a) an AI voice receptionist that answers the Customer's phone calls and books/manages appointments; and (b) a web dashboard for managing the calendar, patients, appointments, and call records (the "Service").

1.2. The specific plan, included usage, and features are set out in the **Order Form / Schedule 1**.

1.3. We may improve or modify the Service over time, provided we do not materially reduce core functionality during a paid term.

### 2. Term & Renewal
2.1. This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for an initial term of `[1 month / 12 months]` ("Initial Term").

2.2. It then **auto-renews** for successive periods of `[1 month / 12 months]` unless either Party gives `[30]` days' written notice before the end of the current term.

2.3. **Trial/Pilot (if applicable):** A `[30]`-day `[free / discounted]` pilot applies from the start date. Either Party may end the pilot with `[7]` days' notice.

### 3. Fees & Payment
3.1. The Customer pays the fees in **Schedule 1** (the "Fees").

3.2. **Setup fee:** `[€___]`, one-time, due on signing `[waived if annual]`.

3.3. **Subscription fee:** `[€___] per month`, billed `[monthly / annually in advance]`.

3.4. **Usage overage:** Calls beyond the included `[___]` minutes/month are billed at `[€___] per minute`.

3.5. Invoices are due within `[14]` days. Late amounts may accrue interest at `[statutory rate]` and we may suspend the Service after `[14]` days' written notice of non-payment.

3.6. Fees are **exclusive of VAT**, which is added where applicable.

3.7. We may increase Fees for a renewal term with at least `[30]` days' notice before renewal.

### 4. Customer Responsibilities
The Customer shall:
- 4.1. Provide accurate clinic information (hours, providers, services, FAQs) needed to configure the Service.
- 4.2. Keep login credentials secure and ensure only authorized staff access the dashboard.
- 4.3. **Review the AI's bookings and call records** as part of normal operations — the Service supports staff, it does not replace clinical or front-desk judgment.
- 4.4. Display/communicate a **patient privacy notice** stating that calls may be handled by an AI assistant and recorded, and obtain any patient consents required for call recording and automated handling (we provide template wording and guidance; the clinic owns the patient relationship).
- 4.4a. Keep the **in-call AI disclosure enabled** — the voice agent identifies the clinic, the purpose of the call, and that it is an AI assistant (e.g. *"I'm the [Clinic] virtual assistant"*). The Customer must not disable or alter this disclosure.
- 4.5. Use the Service lawfully and not for spam, harassment, or unlawful calls.

### 5. Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR)
5.1. In handling personal data of patients/callers, the **Customer is the Data Controller** and **we are the Data Processor**.

5.2. The Parties will sign the **Data Processing Agreement (Schedule 2)**, which governs how we process personal data, including **call recordings, transcripts, and health-related information**, strictly on the Customer's documented instructions.

5.3. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (encryption in transit, access controls, backups, restricted staff access).

5.4. We will not sell patient data and will only use sub-processors (e.g. voice/AI, hosting, telephony providers) listed in the DPA, under equivalent data-protection terms.

5.5. On termination, we will delete or return personal data per the DPA, subject to legal retention requirements.

### 6. Service Levels & Support
6.1. We target **`[99.5%]` monthly uptime**, excluding scheduled maintenance and third-party outages (telephony, AI provider) outside our reasonable control.

6.2. Support is provided via `[email / portal]` during `[business hours / 24x7]` with target first response of `[1 business day / 4 hours]` per the plan.

6.3. Scheduled maintenance will be notified in advance where practical.

### 7. AI Disclaimer & Acceptable Reliance
7.1. The AI voice agent uses automated speech and language models. While designed to be accurate, **it may occasionally misunderstand, mis-book, or fail to handle a call**, and third-party AI/telephony services can have outages.

7.2. The Service is an **operational support tool**. It does **not** provide medical, dental, or clinical advice, and must not be relied on for emergencies. The Customer remains responsible for clinical decisions and for verifying critical appointments.

7.3. We provide the Service on a commercially reasonable basis and do not warrant it will be error-free or uninterrupted.

### 8. Intellectual Property
8.1. We own all IP in the Service, software, and underlying technology. The Customer receives a **non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use** the Service during the term.

8.2. The Customer owns its own data (patient records, call logs). We may use **aggregated, anonymized** data to improve the Service.

8.3. The Customer grants us the right to use its name/logo as a reference customer `[only with prior written consent / unless opted out]`.

### 9. Confidentiality
Each Party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. This survives termination.

### 10. Liability
10.1. Nothing limits liability for death/personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything that cannot be limited by law.

10.2. Subject to 10.1, **neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential losses** (including lost profits, lost patients, or lost revenue).

10.3. Subject to 10.1, each Party's **total aggregate liability** in any 12-month period is capped at the **total Fees paid by the Customer in that period**.

### 11. Termination
11.1. Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within `[30]` days of written notice.

11.2. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent.

11.3. On termination: access ends, outstanding Fees fall due, and data is handled per the DPA (Section 5).

### 12. General
12.1. **Governing law:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of `[Hungary / your jurisdiction]`, and the courts of `[CITY]` have exclusive jurisdiction.

12.2. **Entire agreement:** This document plus its Schedules is the whole agreement and supersedes prior discussions.

12.3. **Changes:** Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.4. **Assignment:** Neither Party may assign without the other's consent, except to a successor of its business.

12.5. **Force majeure:** Neither Party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

## Schedule 2 — Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

> A full DPA is required and is too detailed to inline here. It must cover, at minimum:
> - Subject matter, duration, nature & purpose of processing
> - Categories of data subjects (patients, callers) and data (contact details, **health data**, call recordings, transcripts)
> - Processor obligations (process only on instructions, confidentiality, security measures)
> - **List of sub-processors** (e.g. Retell/voice AI, hosting/Hetzner, telephony, email)
> - International transfer safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses, if data leaves the EU — relevant if your AI provider is US-based)
> - Data subject rights assistance, breach notification timelines, audit rights
> - Deletion/return of data on termination
>
> **Have your lawyer prepare or review this — it is the highest-risk part of selling to healthcare clinics.**

## Practical notes for you (delete before sending)

- **Keep the first contracts short and simple.** Monthly term + 30-day notice lowers the barrier to your first "yes." Push annual terms once you have proof.
- **The DPA is non-negotiable for dental/healthcare.** Don't sign your first clinic without one — a data breach involving patient health data is the one mistake that can sink the business.
- **Liability cap = fees paid** is standard and protects you; expect larger clinics to negotiate it.
- **Check where your AI provider stores/processes data.** If Retell or your LLM processes EU patient data in the US, you need transfer safeguards (SCCs) in the DPA.
- **A simple one-page Order Form** (Schedule 1) is often all a small clinic actually reads — make it clear and friendly.
