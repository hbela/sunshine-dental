# Cold Email & Outreach Sequence — Sunshine Dental

*A 5-touch sequence for reaching dental practice owners and office managers. Personalize the brackets `[ ]` before sending. Keep emails short — most are read on a phone between patients.*

**Target:** Dentist owner / practice manager at independent and small-group dental clinics.
**Goal of every email:** book a 10-minute demo.
**Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 6 → Day 10 → Day 14 (then move to nurture).

## Tips for sending

- **Personalize line 1.** Reference their clinic, a recent review, or that you called and got voicemail (proof of the problem).
- **Send Tue–Thu, mornings.** Avoid Mondays and Friday afternoons.
- **One CTA per email** — always the demo link. Never add a second ask.
- **Plain text beats fancy.** No images or heavy formatting in cold email — it hurts deliverability.
- **Track replies, not opens.** A reply (even "no thanks") is the real signal.
- **Stop on reply.** Pull anyone who responds out of the sequence immediately.
