# Follow-Up Nurture Sequence — Sunshine Dental

*For prospects who saw the [5-touch cold sequence](outreach-emails.md) but didn't book a demo. The goal here shifts: stop pushing for the meeting, start building trust. Give value, stay top-of-mind, and let them come back when the timing is right.*

**Audience:** Engaged-but-not-ready prospects (opened, maybe clicked, didn't book) and polite "not now" replies.
**Goal:** Stay warm and credible until their timing changes — then convert.
**Cadence:** Roughly every 10–14 days. Slower and lighter than cold outreach. One useful idea per email.
**Tone:** Helpful peer, not salesperson. Most emails give value without asking for the demo.

## Running the nurture track — notes

- **Value-to-ask ratio ~3:1.** Most emails should give something useful before any mention of a demo. That's what keeps you welcome in the inbox.
- **One idea per email.** If you're tempted to add a second point, save it for the next send.
- **Move fast on buying signals.** A reply, a pricing question, or a site visit means switch back to direct booking mode immediately — don't keep them on the drip.
- **Refresh the proof.** Replace the generic case study in Email 3 with a real, named result the moment you have one.
- **Quarterly check-ins after this.** If they finish all 6 and stay quiet, drop to a light once-a-quarter "here's what's new" touch.
- **Always honor opt-outs.** A clean unsubscribe protects deliverability for everyone else.
